Amell Gym in Dar es Salaam

Transform Your Body, Elevate Your Lifestyle

Your Journey to Better Health Starts Here

Amell Gym is Gwambina’s urban fitness centre at PSSSF Tower on Sam Nujoma Road, Dar es Salaam — the same lifestyle hub as Amell dining, pool and events. It is for members, hotel-style guests and professionals who want a proper workout in the city, not for mining-camp tenders.

Our experienced fitness professionals are committed to supporting members of all fitness levels through personalized guidance, modern facilities, and an inspiring atmosphere that encourages success.

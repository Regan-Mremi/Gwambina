Waste Management for Camps, Factories and Institutions in Tanzania

Creating Cleaner, Healthier & Sustainable Communities

Reliable Waste Management Solutions

Gwambina Group handles waste as part of running a safe camp or facility — not as a truck that appears when bins overflow. We collect, segregate, move and dispose of waste for mining camps, industrial sites, corporates, hotels and institutions in Tanzania, in line with environmental and public-health rules.

On a remote site, waste is an HSE finding waiting to happen: food waste next to the kitchen, mixed medical or workshop waste in the same skip, burning at the fence line, or a contractor who cannot show where the load went. We close that gap.

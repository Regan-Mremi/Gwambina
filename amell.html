About

Amell in Dar

The Ultimate Lifestyle Hub in the Heart of Dar es Salaam

Conveniently located at PSSSF Tower on Sam Nujoma Road, Amell in Dar is more than just a venue—it is a multi-joint lifestyle destination designed for relaxation, wellness, and celebration. Whether you are stopping by for a business lunch, an energizing workout, or a vibrant weekend night out, Amell delivers an unparalleled experience in a sophisticated, welcoming atmosphere.

Enjoy the Best View


Escape the ordinary and experience breathtaking panoramic views from one of Dar es Salaam's most unique destinations. Whether you're joining us for a delicious meal, relaxing with friends, or celebrating a special occasion, every moment is elevated by a stunning atmosphere designed to create unforgettable memories.

Your Fitness, Your Lifestyle


Stay committed to your fitness goals in our modern, fully equipped gym. From strength training to cardio workouts, our facilities provide everything you need to stay active, healthy, and energized while enjoying your time at Amell in Dar

Where Music Comes Alive

Experience the perfect blend of live music, exciting entertainment, and an electric atmosphere. From talented DJs and live bands to unforgettable themed nights and special events, every visit promises great vibes, amazing performances, and memories that last long after the music ends.

Fine Dining & Craft Cocktails with a View

Indulge in culinary excellence at Amell's signature restaurant and bar. Our chefs blend local Tanzanian flavors with international cuisine, using fresh ingredients to craft every dish to perfection.
